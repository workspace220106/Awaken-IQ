How We Started

Awaken IQ idea began 10 years ago, when the founders embarked on their parenting journey and witnessed firsthand the immense untapped potential of the human brain. Their search for ways to nurture this potential led them to enroll their own children in various brain booster programs, through which their kids began to stand out academically and personally.

They realized that while formal degrees often reflect Intelligent Quotient (IQ), true success in life is largely driven by Emotional Quotient (EQ) and deeper cognitive abilities. This inspired a mission to understand the full potential of the left and right brain functions.

Over time, ancient practices started to unfold as powerful tools in enhancing brain function:

  • Sound therapy and music vibrations
  • Yogic kriyas & dance
  • Sadhana , silence , devotion and visualization
  • Neuro activities
